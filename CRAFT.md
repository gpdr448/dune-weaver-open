# Craft

The rules in MACHINE.md make a file valid. These notes are about making it good.

**A drawing lives one day.** It is seen at breakfast in slanting light, flat at
noon, raked at evening, and then erased by the next clear. Design for the whole
day: something that reads at noon from density alone and wakes up in low light
from its ridges. Erasure is part of the medium, not its failure.

**The field is small.** Its radius is about fifteen line-widths. Empty sand is
cheap and beautiful — the clear's fine rings are already a texture, a ground to
compose against. Most good works use a few strong gestures and leave room.
Crowding costs more than it gives: past a point everything fuses into mush.

**One line is the constraint.** The ball never lifts, so every connector is
drawn. Make connectors earn their place — run them along the rim as a border,
hide them in a retrace, turn them into the subject. A work that feels like one
gesture usually beats one that feels like several pieces stitched together.

**Order is depth.** The later stroke lies on top. Plan the sequence the way a
painter plans layers: ground first, figure last; the thing you want to read as
nearest is drawn last.

**The table has features; use them.** The centre is a raised mound no line can
reach — a pupil, a seed, a sleeper's head, a hole. Lines that pass near it part
around it (the eye). The rim band softens anything near the edge. The grain
turns cross-cutting lines into combs and leaves along-grain lines smooth.
Designs that acknowledge these read as belonging to the table.

**Irregular is alive.** Regular sines, equal spacing and perfect symmetry read
as mechanical in sand. Rivers meander unevenly; hands waver; a motif that
grows, fades or drifts slightly each repeat reads as intention. Vary on purpose.

**Contrast of texture beats contrast of shape.** Sand has no colour. What reads
is the difference between a smooth dish and a fingerprint, a knife ridge and a
broad bank, a combed band and an untouched one. Plan the textures, not just
the outlines.

**End deliberately.** The ball stays where the file ends until the next clear —
it is visible all day. Park it somewhere that means something (a spiral in, a
stop at the rim, the end of a line of thought).

**Check the whole picture.** Look at the honest preview and the simulation
together, at small size, as if across a room. If it does not read there, it
will not read on the table.
